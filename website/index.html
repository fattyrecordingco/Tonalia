Pick the sound.
Leave the rest.

Time runs from left to right. Deep sounds sit near the bottom; bright, high sounds sit near the top. Brighter colours show stronger sounds.

Draw around a whistle, hum or other sound with a rectangle, lasso or brush. Turn it down, listen, and adjust. Your change becomes a layer you can switch off later.

Make your first edit

Hear the change.
Keep the choice.

Switch between Original and Result to compare. Use Removed to hear what your edit is taking away.

Save a project to keep your editable layers. Export a WAV to share the finished audio. Keep the original recording alongside your project.

Saving and exporting explained

Try an edit
before you install.

The browser editor can open a local WAV, make selections, add reversible edits, play the result and export a new file. Your recording is processed on your computer.

Open browser editor

Use a computer-sized window. You need a connection to load the editor; install Tonalia for offline use. Local-network sharing is available in the desktop app.

A safe place to start

  1. Open the editor.
  2. Choose Explore a calibration session.
  3. Try an edit on the built-in practice sound.

No recording to hand? The practice sound is generated in the app. It isn’t someone else’s session.

What this version does.

Tonalia 0.2.0 edits mono and stereo WAV recordings. It includes frequency selections, reversible volume changes, initial clean-up tools, offline speech denoising and experimental sharing on a local network.

It doesn’t yet open MP3s, record audio, host plug-ins or provide a full multitrack timeline. Read the current limits before using it on an important job.
