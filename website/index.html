New in the desktop app

Find the sources.
Give them their own track.

Open Separate sounds → Auto separate. Tonalia analyses your recording, detects likely sources and extracts WAV tracks with spectral cleanup to reduce bleed. Music can be split into vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and other instruments.

Find sounds suggests what the analysis detects. Preview a track, open it for closer editing or export it. Tiny clicks have their own transient tool; unassigned audio stays in a residual track. Results are estimates: listen and compare.

Set up local sound separation

Pick the sound.
Leave the rest.

Time runs from left to right. Deep sounds sit near the bottom; bright, high sounds sit near the top. Brighter colours show stronger sounds.

Zoom into a frequency region, then scroll or drag vertically to follow it. Paint with a continuous brush and adjustable size, opacity and hardness. Build one selection with multiple strokes, rectangles and lassos, then refine it with the eraser. Click Create gain layer when you’re ready; each layer can be switched off later.

Make your first edit

Hear the change.
Keep the choice.

Switch between Original and Result to compare. Use Removed to hear what your edit is taking away.

Export Result for the cleaned recording and Removed for the spectral material you took away. Save a project too, so your layers stay editable. Keep the original WAV alongside it.

Saving and exporting explained

Try an edit
before you install.

Drop a WAV into the browser editor, zoom and scroll through its spectrum, paint a layer or capture a noise profile. Compare and export Original, Result or Removed. Your recording is processed on your computer.

Open browser editor

Use a computer-sized window. You need a connection to load the editor; install Tonalia for offline use. AI sound separation and local-network sharing require the desktop app.

What this version does.

Tonalia 0.5.0 edits mono and stereo WAV recordings. It adds automatic sound discovery and separated WAV tracks on desktop, captured noise profiles, precise frequency navigation, combined selections, a selection eraser, improved brush performance and separate Removed-signal export.

It doesn’t yet open MP3s, record audio, host plug-ins or provide a full multitrack timeline. Read the current limits before using it on an important job.
